# Product

## Register

product

The signed-in app (Home, Discover, Search, Library, the reader, Ask, settings, admin) is product UI. The public landing page and the sign-in and register pages are brand surfaces: treat them with the brand register when they are the task.

## Users

Readers who follow stories across formats: comics, graphic novels, manga, manhwa, manhua, light novels, novels, webcomics and nonfiction. They come to find the next thing to read, keep track of what they are reading, and read public-domain or their own DRM-free books in the browser. Most sessions are leisure: evenings, on a laptop or a phone, often in a dim room. Admins run the catalog from a separate shell.

## Product Purpose

Bookflix brings every medium into one catalog, recommends what to read next from a reader's own activity, and lets them read in the app where the book is public domain, owned or licensed (official "Where to read" links otherwise). Success is a reader who opens Bookflix, finds something they want within a minute, and comes back to continue it.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, premium, confident. It borrows the feel of a streaming service (dark canvas, covers as the art, full-bleed bands) without copying one. Copy is short, specific and sure of itself. Red is reserved for calls to action; the featured gradient (navy to maroon to dark red) marks the moments the product wants to celebrate, starting with Trending Now.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI or SaaS templates: gradient blobs, glassmorphism, emoji, icon-in-circle feature rows, centered-everything layouts, filler copy.
- Cheap themes: oversized buttons, full-bleed inputs, inset rounded boxes where a band should run edge to edge.
- Any Netflix asset: no Netflix logos, posters or Netflix Sans. Inspiration only.

## Design Principles

1. Covers are the art: imagery comes from the catalog, and every missing cover has a designed fallback.
2. One focal point and one primary action per screen, with a deliberate type hierarchy.
3. Earned familiarity: standard controls at standard sizes (40 to 44px), the same component vocabulary on every screen.
4. Restraint is the brand: dark-only tokens, red only for actions, the featured gradient only where something is featured.
5. Fast and steady: reserved image sizes, no layout shift, motion only to show state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every control has a 44px target and the shared electric-blue focus ring. Text meets 4.5:1 and UI parts 3:1 against their surface. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Never rely on colour alone.
