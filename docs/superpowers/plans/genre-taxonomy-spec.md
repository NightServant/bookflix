# Bookflix genre taxonomy (user spec, 2026-10-28)

The user's specification for genre selection and discovery. It covers mainstream, niche, international, mature and controversial entertainment literature across comic books, graphic novels, manga, manhwa, manhua, novels, light novels, picture books, visual novels and webcomics.

**Structure.** The taxonomy has hierarchical genres, multi-select subgenres, and independent facets. A single work can belong to several categories at once.

Format of the lists below: `## Genre` is a parent genre, `### Group` is a display group inside it, and the items are subgenres, separated by `;`. Sections 17–21 are not genres: they are independent facets.

- **HARD RULE:** sexual content involving minors, and sexualized depictions of minors, must never be supported. Never add such a label, and keep rejecting such records at ingestion.
- Mature works stay subject to legal, platform and age-access restrictions. Access restrictions are independent of filter preferences: a filter never unlocks gated content.

## 22. Selection behaviour (filter UI)
- Expandable parent genres with nested subgenres. Multi-select across genre families.
- Selecting a parent genre includes its descendants. Selecting a subgenre does not select its siblings.
- OR logic within a filter group, AND logic between groups.
- Keep these independent of each other:
  - media type
  - genre and subgenre
  - themes
  - tropes
  - demographics
  - audience ratings
  - content warnings
- Search within the taxonomy, for the large lists.
- Selected filters show as removable chips, with Clear All and Apply Filters actions.
- Users can exclude selected content warnings.
- Access restrictions stay independent of filter preferences.
- A title can have multiple genres (for example fantasy, romance and psychological drama at once).

## Final recommendation
Treat this taxonomy as a broad discovery vocabulary, not a mandatory checklist for every title. Each work gets only the labels that fit, and the taxonomy stays extensible as Bookflix adds publishers, languages, formats and storytelling traditions.
